ACADEX is committed to safeguarding your personal data in compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). This law protects individual personal information in information and communications systems in the government and the private sector, ensuring that personal data is secured and protected.

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data.
  • Correct inaccuracies in your information.
